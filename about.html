About N.K.Enterprises

Life insurance help and festive lighting, from people you can call directly.

Surendra Kumar, Authorised LIC Agent

LIC agency code: 1728/51D · बीमा सलाहकार (insurance advisor)

Surendra Kumar is an authorised agent of Life Insurance Corporation of India. He helps families choose life cover, child education and marriage plans, retirement and pension plans, and savings plans, and he stays with them after the policy is issued.

Most of his work happens after the sale: reminding clients before premiums are due, helping revive lapsed policies, arranging policy loans, updating nominees, and guiding families through maturity and death claims until the money is paid.

You can ask to see his LIC agent ID card when you meet him. His agency code is printed on it.

How we work

  • Direct and personal: you talk to Surendra ji, not a call centre.
  • Plain language: plans are explained in simple Hindi or English, with the premium and benefits side by side.
  • No pressure: advice is free, and you only pay the premium for a policy you choose.
  • Reminders: clients get a WhatsApp message before each premium due date.

What you'll find on this website

  • Insurance tips: plain-language guides on premiums, claims, loans, nominees, tax and choosing a plan.
  • Cover check: a quick way to estimate how much life cover your family needs.
  • Diwali lights: festive lights from our shop, ordered over WhatsApp.
  • Festival wish cards: free cards with your photo to share with family.

About our articles

Our guides explain common LIC questions in simple words. They are general information, not advice for your specific policy. Rules and charges can differ between plans and can change, so always check your policy document or licindia.in, or ask us. If you spot a mistake, please tell us on WhatsApp and we'll correct it.
