How to check your LIC policy status online

Many families keep their LIC policy bonds safely in a cupboard and then are not sure whether the policy is running, when the next premium is due, or how much bonus has been added. You don't need to visit the branch to find out. With a few minutes of setup, you can check everything from your phone.

What you can see online

Once your policies are linked to your online account, you can usually see:

  • Whether each policy is in force, lapsed or paid-up
  • The next premium due date and amount
  • Your premium payment history and receipts
  • Bonus details for with-profit plans
  • Loan eligibility and any loan outstanding
  • Maturity date and nominee details

The exact screens change from time to time as LIC updates its website, but this information is generally available.

Before you start: keep these ready

  • Your policy number (printed on the policy bond and on premium receipts)
  • The instalment premium amount without GST, as written in the policy
  • Your date of birth as recorded in the policy
  • A working mobile number and email address for the one-time password (OTP)

A common reason people get stuck is entering the premium amount including tax, or a date of birth that differs from the one in the policy. Use the details exactly as they appear on your bond.

Step 1: Register on the LIC customer portal

  1. Open LIC's official website, licindia.in. Always type the address yourself instead of clicking links in messages.
  2. Look for the Customer Portal and choose the option for new user registration.
  3. Enter one policy number, the premium amount, your date of birth, mobile number and email.
  4. Create a user ID and password, then confirm using the OTP sent to your phone or email.

You only need to do this once. Keep your password private and never share the OTP with anyone, including someone claiming to be from LIC.

Step 2: Add all your policies

After you log in, use the option to enrol or add policies. Add every policy in your name. Parents often have policies for each child, and it helps to see them all in one place. If a policy will not add, the details you entered may not match LIC's records; your agent or branch can help correct this.

Step 3: Check the status

Open the list of enrolled policies and select one. You will see its current status and key dates. Look carefully at the status line:

  • In force means premiums are up to date and your full cover is active.
  • Lapsed means a premium was not paid even after the grace period. Your full cover has stopped and you should look at revival quickly.
  • Paid-up means premiums stopped after the policy had acquired a paid-up value, so it continues with a reduced sum assured.

Other ways to check

LIC mobile app: LIC has an official app that lets you view policies and pay premiums after logging in. Download it only from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, and check that the publisher is Life Insurance Corporation of India.

SMS and helpline: LIC also offers SMS services and a customer call centre. The current numbers and SMS formats are listed on the "Contact us" section of licindia.in. Please take them from there rather than from forwarded messages, which are often out of date.

Your branch or agent: If you are not comfortable with phones and websites, that is completely fine. Your agent can check the status for you within minutes.

Stay safe from fraud

Fraudsters sometimes call policyholders saying a bonus is "stuck" or a policy will be "cancelled" unless money is paid. LIC does not ask you to deposit money into personal accounts to release bonuses. If a call or message feels strange, hang up and check your status yourself on the official website, or call your agent.
