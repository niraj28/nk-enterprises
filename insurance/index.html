Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge for advice?

No. Talking through your needs and comparing plans is free. You only pay the premium for the policy you choose.

I already have an LIC policy from another agent. Can you help?

Yes. I can help with premium payments, revivals, nomination changes, loans and claims on existing policies too.

How do you remind me about premiums?

I send a WhatsApp message before each due date, so you never miss the grace period.

What do I need to buy a policy?

Usually an ID and address proof, age proof, a photo, and bank details. For higher cover, a medical check-up may be needed. I'll tell you exactly what applies to you.

Can you help with a claim after a death in the family?

Yes, and I know this is a hard time. I'll guide the nominee through every form and document, and follow up until the claim is settled.
