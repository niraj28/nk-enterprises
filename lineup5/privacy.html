Line Up 5 privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. Applies to the game at digitallifenotes.blog/lineup5/ and the Line Up 5 iPhone app.

In short: no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking and no ads in the app. Your games, record and badges stay on your device. A few things are sent to our game server only when you use online features, and AI players send game details to Google Gemini.

Stored on your device

  • Your settings (players, mode, AI level, sound on or off), a game in progress so you can continue it, and your record: games played and won, streaks, fastest win, badges and daily challenge results.
  • A random player ID, used only to keep one daily leaderboard entry per player, and a seat key for online rooms so you can rejoin.

This stays in the browser's or app's local storage. Deleting the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes it.

Online rooms and quick match

When you play online, the game server (run on Cloudflare) receives the name you type, your moves, and on the website any chat messages and emoji reactions you send. Other players in the room see your name, moves, chat and reactions. A room is deleted 2 hours after it was last used.

Daily challenge leaderboard

Only if you tap Post my result: the server receives the name you type, your random player ID and your moves for that day's game, replays the game to check the result, and shows your name and result publicly on that day's leaderboard. Entries are deleted after about a week.

AI players and Google Gemini

To choose a move and write their table talk, AI players send our server the board, that AI player's own cards, the moves it can make and recent table talk (which can include chat typed by players). Our server passes this to Google's Gemini API. We don't send your name with it beyond what appears in the table talk, and we don't send device identifiers. We use Google's free tier, under which Google may use this content to improve its products. See Google's Gemini API terms. The daily challenge never uses Gemini.

Sharing and notifications (iPhone app)

Sharing a result or invite opens the iPhone share sheet; nothing is sent until you choose an app to share with. The daily reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn it off in the game or in iPhone Settings.

The website

On the website the game page is part of digitallifenotes.blog, which shows Google AdSense ads and loads fonts from Google Fonts. See the site privacy policy for how those work. The iPhone app has no ads.

Children

The game doesn't ask for age, email or contact details. The iPhone app has no typed chat in online games, only preset emoji reactions.
