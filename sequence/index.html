Players

Tap AI or Human for each seat. Turns go 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.

    How to play
    • 2 vs 2: Blue (seats 1, 3) plays Green (seats 2, 4), 6 cards each, 2 sequences to win. 1 vs 1: 7 cards each, 2 sequences. 3 players: everyone for themselves, 6 cards each, 1 sequence wins.
    • Pick a card from your hand, then tap one of its two matching spaces to drop your team's chip.
    • J♦ J♣ are wild: place a chip on any open space.
    • J♠ J♥ take away one opponent chip (not one in a finished line).
    • 5 in a row in any direction is a sequence. The ★ corners count for everyone.
    • First team to 2 sequences wins. Cards with no open space are swapped automatically.
    AI connection (site owner)

    AI seats ask Google Gemini through the site's Cloudflare Worker. If Gemini is busy, they use the free built-in brain. The API key stays in the Worker.
